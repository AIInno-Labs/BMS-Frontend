FRP Engineering — Job Card PDF Field Guide

What fills automatically from the app vs what is completed manually on the shop floor.

How to fill & export

  1. Open the job (e.g. JOB-Q-17843) in BMSMan.
  2. Click Edit details.
  3. Scroll to Official job card (PDF fields) — fill specs, clips, packs, notes, etc.
  4. On the main job card, set QA completed if QA name should appear on export.
  5. Click Save, then Export PDF.
